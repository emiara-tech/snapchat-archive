# Goodbye Chat

Goodbye Chat turns a person's Snapchat export into a connected account of their conversations, media, and history. These terms distinguish preserved evidence, personal curation, and imagined interpretations.

## Language

### Evidence

**Original archive**: The unchanged export files supplied by the archive owner. They are the source of evidence for everything reconstructed in Goodbye Chat.
_Avoid_: Backup database, uploaded account

**Archive owner**: The person whose Snapchat account produced the original archive. Their authored messages are the evidence used to construct an imagined self.
_Avoid_: Sender, protagonist

**Source record**: A single record or file occurrence in the original archive, including its original context. A source record can be incomplete, duplicated, or inconsistent with another source record.
_Avoid_: Ground truth, reconstructed message

**Dataset**: The connected interpretation of source records that the owner explores. It preserves the evidence behind its items and relationships.
_Avoid_: Original archive, database dump

**Participant**: A person or account appearing in a conversation. A participant's display name alone does not establish their identity.
_Avoid_: Friend, chat

**Conversation**: The ordered exchange involving a defined set of participants, including its text and associated media. A conversation can include gaps where the export lacks content.
_Avoid_: Chat history file, person

**Conversation event**: A text message, media message, or other recorded occurrence within a conversation. Events retain their source, authorship, and available time information.
_Avoid_: Database row, snap

**Media asset**: A photo, video, audio recording, sticker, or other media item represented in the archive. The same asset can appear in more than one conversation event.
_Avoid_: Message, attachment occurrence

**Media overlay**: A separate visual layer associated with a media asset, such as a caption, drawing, or sticker. It is part of reconstructing the asset's appearance.
_Avoid_: Original image, watermark

**Media link**: A relationship between a media asset and its conversation event or other archive context. Its evidence can make it confirmed, inferred, ambiguous, or unlinked.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed match, guessed fact

**Coverage**: The portions of an owner's history actually represented in the archive. Missing records do not establish that an event never happened.
_Avoid_: Complete history, inactivity

### Curation

**Review decision**: An owner's reversible choice about an item, such as keeping it, excluding it, or marking it for later review. It does not change the original archive.
_Avoid_: Deletion, source edit

**Curated collection**: The items selected by filters and review decisions for a particular purpose. An export, analysis, or persona uses an explicitly identified collection.
_Avoid_: Original archive, everything

**Export bundle**: A portable copy of an explicitly chosen complete dataset or curated collection, including selected media and context. Its stated selection and export policy define which available evidence it preserves; missing source content remains missing.
_Avoid_: Original archive, cloud backup

**Composed media**: A separate rendered copy of a media asset with its supported overlays combined into its visible appearance. It retains a reference to the preserved original asset and overlay layers.
_Avoid_: Original image, restored original

**Destination transfer**: An explicitly authorized copy of selected supported media to the owner's chosen external account or service. Its result can be partial and does not by itself preserve the conversations or other context of an export bundle.
_Avoid_: Complete archive backup, synchronization

### Interpretation

**Observation**: A result supported by recorded evidence, such as a message count within a defined period. Its scope and coverage are part of its meaning.
_Avoid_: Personality trait, assumption

**Interpretation**: An explanation or hypothesis about observations. It may help the owner reflect, but it remains distinct from recorded evidence.
_Avoid_: Proven fact, diagnosis

**Year profile**: A description of the archive owner's observed language and context for a chosen year and curated collection. Its evidence and uncertainty accompany its conclusions.
_Avoid_: Complete personality, trained person

**Imagined self**: A fictional conversational persona informed by a year profile. It can reflect observed language without becoming the actual past person or possessing their unrecorded memories.
_Avoid_: Digital twin, recovered self, real past self
