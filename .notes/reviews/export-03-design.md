# EXPORT-03 design accepted

Foundation authored the [recoverable local-bundle contract](../tasks/export-03.md). Root independently reviewed it against [Phase 6](../specs/phase-6.md), the destination contract, accepted EXPORT-02 corrections, current writer/snapshot interfaces and the query/filter/rules/media dependencies. Both design axes have no blocking finding; no distinct correction was necessary. This accepts bounded design only.

The contract preserves one frozen effective population, scoped generated context/provenance and unchanged originals. Per-target receipts distinguish generated bytes, offered downloads, acknowledged writes, verified saved files, missing/unsupported items, failures and unknown outcomes. Explicit unchanged-authority retry reconciles possible duplicate handoff before proceeding. A partial repair cannot claim a complete collection without the exact reopened part set.

Reopening verifies actual entries against independent expected bytes, context and pixels. Present bundle evidence stays separate from declared provenance for absent original documents. No original QueryEvidence, source lookup or review authority is manufactured from the manifest.

Before code, freeze concrete application interfaces and shared ownership against accepted runtime producers, including ordering authority. Freeze finite metadata, catalogue/index, receipt and verifier budgets with a complete partition/root/checksum contract. Draft page/chunk targets and existing ZIP ceilings do not establish heap support.

In-product ingestion, if implemented, requires its own versioned format, bundle-specific evidence/authority and supported adapter. Offline readable context/composition reopening remains required. Actual large raw-import/browser measurements, realistic private round-trip, codec/video limits, destination transfers and release remain open. No code, tests, private data, external service or transfer was used for this design review.
