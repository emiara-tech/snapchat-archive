# Phase 12: A coherent verified release

## User value and full gate

A fresh visitor can understand the promise, request an archive, return with it, rediscover a connected memory, curate and export a useful collection, inspect an observation, and try an explicitly imagined year self through their own authorized AI access. The same terms, selection, source inspector, and failure behavior connect every room.

The full gate is the supported complete journey on the actual deployment, accurate public claims and limitations, verified operational recovery, and privacy-safe release evidence. Core journey acceptance, genuine ChatGPT integration, supported Google Photos/Immich transfers, reminders across a real wait, and authorized inference retain their earlier gates. A polished local preview cannot be labelled a complete production release. Sources are [MASTERPLAN.md](../../MASTERPLAN.md#phase-12-finish-the-product-and-bring-it-to-users), [agents.md](../../agents.md), [auth.md](../../auth.md), [docs/onboarding.md](../../docs/onboarding.md), [docs/export-destinations.md](../../docs/export-destinations.md), and [docs/visualization.md](../../docs/visualization.md).

## Current baseline and prerequisites

The repository has pnpm build, unit, and browser scripts, a local archive reader, a Memories browser, and early statistics. Its landing page currently promises destination exports and calls example counts a streak despite those full journeys not being implemented. Existing marketing and type declarations are not delivery evidence.

Release preparation can begin with local verification, actual archive imports, coherent controls, and honest copy. Passing the complete release gate depends on every required earlier journey, deployment access, configured production callbacks, provider approvals, authorized connected accounts, destination access, and the real waiting interval for reminder verification. Those prerequisites do not become completed because synthetic equivalents work.

## Coherent product journey

Use the domain language in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md). Arrival offers the genuine Snapchat request link and immediate local import. Returning visitors can import without an account. Sign-in, reminders, destination transfer, AI allowance, and external-processing consent remain independent controls.

After import, coverage and a private source-backed reveal lead into rediscovery, analysis, or curation. Every route enters the same workspace. Year, participant, conversation, review context, and selected item persist where useful. An excluded item cannot return through another room's thumbnail, query, statistics example, profile, or export preview.

The conversation inspector and media detail preserve evidence states. Keep/exclude/later decisions stay reversible. Export explains complete versus curated scope and promised originals/compositions. Destination transfers name the authorized account or instance, supported formats, and partial results. Statistics and 3D scenes explain population, denominator, coverage, and visual encoding. The year room shows fiction, source year, approved evidence, funding, and cancellation throughout.

Empty, missing, loading, incomplete, and failed states explain what the owner can do next. Progress corresponds to actual work. A failure in optional authentication, reminders, destinations, AI, or GPU rendering cannot destroy local archive access. Account sessions never imply cloud archive backup.

## Truthful discovery and feedback

Automated tests import reproducible internal synthetic ZIP fixtures with mixed media, connected conversations, overlays, unresolved assets, exclusions, timezone boundaries, multilingual owner text and sparse years. Verify actual curation, export and year-profile behavior through the product’s real import path. Do not ship a public fixture mode or canned persona conversation.

Landing and launch copy describe completed verified features and supported cases. Remove unsupported claims about universal destinations, complete recovered history, Snapchat streaks, ChatGPT plan access, total account balances, or data never leaving the device when an opt-in remote feature exists. Explain that local archive work stays local and approved external features disclose their packets and recipients.

Provide a feedback path with a small diagnostic packet the owner can inspect before submission. Default diagnostics contain app revision, browser/backend capability, operation/error codes, and safe resource information. They contain no archive files, names, messages, paths, precise locations, profiles, transcripts, credentials, or screenshots of private records. Allow a text report without uploading a private archive. Do not automatically post a user's report publicly.

Prepare a launch page and bounded discovery experiment as reviewable artifacts. Actual outreach, contacting people, publishing posts, recruiting users, and collecting their histories require explicit activity and data boundaries. Existing authorized release/deployment instructions remain applicable; do not invent a new approval gate for routine engineering work.

## Operations and measurement

Verify the deployed revision, direct document routes, static hashed assets, production callbacks, cookie/session behavior, same-origin APIs, and optional service capability states. A browser must open important routes directly and recover when an archive is absent. Stale HTML cannot strand visitors on missing JavaScript. Record a known rollback target and test the recovery procedure without copying private data into evidence.

Exercise login/logout/reload and isolated accounts, revoked AI connection, allowance exhaustion, concurrent requests, interrupted streams, malformed/replaced archives, cancellation, exports, destination partial failure, GPU loss, and reduced motion. Review dependencies and actual update/failure paths. Respect current-head CodeRabbit and branch-protection requirements in [agents.md](../../agents.md); a prior review does not approve later fixes.

Optional outcome measurement needs a disclosed privacy design before collection. Useful outcomes are a successful import, a useful export, an understood observation, and a completed year-room visit. Count only minimal events with documented retention and opt-out behavior. Do not transmit filenames, contact labels, exact personal counts, profile text, selected years linked to identity, or a private conversation merely to measure engagement. Lack of telemetry is preferable to undisclosed collection. Waiting/reminder retention measurements follow the dedicated minimal-data contract.

Operational evidence records app revision, acceptance results, test environment, resource budgets, affected journey, rollback checks, and limitations. It distinguishes mocked success, local browser success, actual-provider success, and deployed success. Temporary tasks and tuning experiments stay in working notes or issues; durable product/privacy conventions belong in documentation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A fresh visitor can follow request, wait, return, local import, reveal, conversation, curation, export, observation, profile, and funded imagined-self journeys on the actual host wherever the supported capabilities are enabled.
2. The real request-to-return interval and consented reminder lifecycle are verified, including cancellation, return/import suppression, duplicate delivery prevention, and restart recovery. A synthetic clock run is labelled separately.
3. Terminology, shared selection, evidence links, and reversible review decisions agree across rooms. No excluded record reappears through an alternate consumer.
4. Complete and curated bundles match their preview and manifest. Google Photos and Immich use separately authorized verified destinations and report supported, failed, and partial results honestly.
5. Genuine production identity and funded synthetic inference are verified. Genuine ChatGPT sign-in/plan usage retains its provider approval requirement and has no misleading enabled substitute.
6. Desktop keyboard access, representative viewports, reduced motion, WebGPU/WebGL capability, no GPU, device loss, and large synthetic benchmark scenarios retain useful controls and meet recorded requirements.
7. Production session/ownership checks, callback denial/replay, wrong-account access, revoked allowance, bounded concurrent spending, stream cancellation, and absence of shared funded fallback pass meaningful integration checks.
8. Damaged import retry, archive replacement/reset, route reload, export cancellation, destination interruption, and stale asset recovery leave no old archive visible and preserve accurate operation states.
9. Public copy accurately lists delivered capabilities, missing-data limitations, destinations, privacy transfers, fictional persona behavior, and external approval gates.
10. Public release evidence, visual baselines, issue evidence, CI, deployment uploads, and routine logs contain only synthetic content or safe metadata. The optional feedback packet is inspectable and excludes private derivatives and secrets.
11. The deployed revision passes applicable pnpm checks, current-head review requirements, direct-route verification, and an exercised operational recovery path. Build success alone cannot satisfy this criterion.
12. Outcome collection, if enabled, has disclosed purpose, fields, retention, recipients, and controls. Prepared discovery artifacts make no claims of actual outreach or user research unless those activities were separately authorized and completed.

## Verification and delivery slices

Run `pnpm run build`, `pnpm run test`, and `pnpm run test:e2e` on the final integrated revision. Use browser assertions for selection, evidence, consent, progress, and recovery alongside synthetic screenshots. Inspect unexpected network requests and browser errors. Real private archive checks remain local and their screenshots/derivatives stay outside tracked paths.

First deliver consistent navigation and honest capability states. Follow with verified real archive journeys, inspected safe feedback, and a launch artifact. Complete real provider, destination, waiting, performance, production, review, and rollback evidence before claiming the full release. Record unresolved external gates precisely and continue independent local work; do not delete masterplan requirements to make the completion report appear finished.
