# SCENE-02: Connected factual archive rooms

Accepted bounded design against [Phase 8](../specs/phase-8.md), MASTERPLAN Phase 8 and [the visualization contract](../../docs/visualization.md). Experience authored it and root independently reviewed both original-spec and standards axes with no blocking finding. [The design review](../reviews/scene-02-design.md) records acceptance and pre-code requirements. Accepted QUERY, OCCURRENCE, ORDERING, FILTER, COVERAGE, RULES, PERFORMANCE, MEDIA and OBSERVATION contracts control the facts, authority and resource boundaries. No code, measurement or complete phase is accepted. The future-session VISUAL-001 cinematic/dark-mode redesign remains separately backlogged.

## Owner outcome and full gate

The owner can see a recorded pattern, select it, open the exact contributing evidence and continue into another room without losing their collection. A year leads to a conversation, its actual media uses and the words behind a measured change. Spatial treatment earns its place by making that path understandable. An unusual shape, repeated photograph or changed word rate is interesting because the owner can verify it.

All five rooms remain required: timeline, relationship constellation, memory gallery, language landscape and map. Each needs a distinct composition, explained factual encodings, the same effective selection and a complete reading/keyboard journey. Passing one room, attractive screenshots or a small fixture does not close Phase 8. Large-history desktop measurement, actual renderer capability and recovery remain full gates.

Keep this slice within the existing Observatory visual language: warm HTML panels, ink/teal procedural spaces, restrained brass accents, readable labels and the owner's permitted local media. Use reproducible Three.js geometry. This task defines useful room composition and transitions, not a new front page, treasure chest, public demonstration, persona model or site-wide style system. No sound starts automatically. Adding optional sound requires explicit local control and licensed/reproducible assets; it is unnecessary for factual-room acceptance.

Each room must make a useful decision or discovery easier, with a visual surprise the records can actually support:

| Room | Owner decision or discovery | Explainable visual surprise and next evidence action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timeline | Choose a period to revisit or curate after seeing where this selected export contains recorded activity. | A genuine burst or gap has actual calendar spacing and named event/occurrence tracks. Select its bin, inspect contributors/coverage and explicitly narrow the shared period. |
| Relationships | Distinguish a frequent proven direct exchange from traffic belonging to a group or unknown conversation. | A large group node can sit separately from direct-recipient nodes with the same display name. Open the count and scope proof, then its real conversation. The layout does not decide relationship quality. |
| Memories | Discover that one photograph has several recorded uses, then decide which appearance or original to inspect or curate. | Repeated frames share a thumbnail yet retain different save/reference times and context. Open the chosen occurrence and its source before taking the existing review action; do not exclude every use by accident. |
| Language | Compare an observed expression under clear corpus sizes before choosing which messages to revisit. | A term's raw count can rise while its normalized rate falls. The paired terrain explains both bases, keeps sparse samples visible and opens the exact term and denominator evidence. |
| Map | Discover which selected saves actually record coordinates and which locations cannot be placed honestly. | Several recorded places may belong to uses of one physical file; a missing-media save may still have a source-backed point. Select the occurrence's own location field, not an inferred journey or a guessed venue. |

When a selected archive lacks the relevant facts, the room explains the limitation and offers its honest evidence/selection action. It does not add data to force these surprises. Existing orbit controls and cards alone do not establish distinct-room usefulness or full acceptance. The later VISUAL-001 overhaul can replace their visual treatment while reusing these application behaviors and evidence targets.

## Current baseline and dependencies

`ArchiveScene.vue` has procedural modes for all five rooms, camera controls, picking, projected HTML labels and a separate persona mode. It draws at most 64 provided nodes, or 18 gallery frames, and labels at most 12. It currently accepts unversioned `SceneNode` values/coordinates/blob URLs directly. It creates individual meshes, and gallery texture acquisition starts from those URLs inside the renderer. It has no validated factual `SceneSpec`, full calculation authority or measured large-history resource contract.

Its existing lifecycle detects a real WebGPU adapter/device, reports the backend actually mounted, catches runtime failures, attempts one WebGL 2 recovery and releases its canvas/resources. It respects reduced motion and pauses while hidden. Existing software-browser recovery checks are useful regressions. They do not establish physical-GPU frame rates, all-room hardware capability or total native-memory bounds.

`ObservatoryView.vue` currently runs the legacy `observationJob`, constructs nodes and derives source/media context in the page. It pages 64 evidence groups, exposes bounded source previews and stores room/group/reading intent in the local route. It acquires gallery thumbnails with a generation guard. `localThumbnail.ts` bounds the edge to 384, source bytes to 32 MiB and concurrent native decoders to three, including canceled decodes still settling. These ownership safeguards must survive the new application adapter.

Legacy observations combine events and standalone asset dates in a timeline, construct gallery/map facts from physical-asset metadata and lack complete occurrence/coverage/metric authority. SCENE-02 must replace those inputs with the accepted projection; it cannot relabel that mixed population as the new contract. The existing coordinate parser accepts only a narrow signed latitude/longitude literal, with an optional `Latitude, Longitude:` prefix. It does not prove a geographic datum, precision, historical accuracy or support for other location shapes.

Before implementation, freeze these concrete prerequisites and assign serial file ownership:

1. Complete runtime OCC evidence, distinct supported reference/Memory occurrences, exact signed times, scoped source/field proof, authored-event and conversation facts, resolved/possible layer distinction and complete capability. Accepted stage-one byte proof alone is insufficient. Do not cast a staged subset to complete QUERY evidence.
2. Accepted ORDERING producer facts and canonical ordering/calculation authority. Gallery and evidence order use that result, including numeric row/reference order for ties and unavailable time. No scene-local timeline parser or sorter.
3. FILTER's current complete effective projection, field-level labels, shared timezone, expected-stamp commands and no-op/prepare/commit semantics. Camera motion, a route and a bare item ID never establish selection authority.
4. COVERAGE's scoped limitation packets and bounded exact source adapter; RULES' effective denies/exceptions and conservative original/layer protection where enabled. Global diagnostics stay deliberate inventory, not scene payloads.
5. Accepted OBSERVATION packets/pages and shared calendar/language methods, including comparison containment, explicit timed unavailable exclusion and per-metric selective authority. Final packet fields are not frozen implementation DTOs yet.
6. MEDIA's exact navigation/lease interfaces and supported codec/overlay boundaries. Scenes reuse those real I/O seams; they do not acquire original bytes, decode an arbitrary URL or invent another media registry.
7. A source-backed occurrence-location projection. It needs an exact target, current evidence authority, original recorded literal/field proof, a validated supported coordinate result or explicit uncertainty, and any actually recorded accuracy facts. Bind this small fact to the final producer/application types. Reuse the existing supported parser under explicit ownership; do not infer from filenames, raw strings in the view, file EXIF, a participant name or an external geocoder.

PERFORMANCE's complete committed collection and bounded worker transport remain in force. The selected population cannot become only a rendered page or cached viewport. If prerequisites are incomplete, say which local capability is unavailable and keep existing permitted navigation usable. Empty factual rooms are preferable to invented history.

## Small application and renderer interfaces

Keep deterministic scene preparation and target resolution outside Vue and Three.js. Proposed public operations, with final names/types frozen against the actual accepted application interfaces before code, are:

```ts
prepareSceneInput(projection, intent)
  -> validated readonly scene input and reading descriptor | safe typed failure

resolveSceneSelection(prepared, target)
  -> exact evidence/navigation/filter-action intent | blocked/stale/unknown result
```

`projection` contains only current permitted observation packets, bounded value/evidence pages, approved occurrence/resource/location facts and matching COVERAGE authority. It is not the raw archive, a participant lookup table or an alternate collection. `intent` chooses one of the five fixed rooms, a permitted metric/group page, bounded camera/style settings and optional current selected target. It never includes a filter predicate, matcher callback, tokenization setting or executable layout. Source/paging/navigation actions reuse the accepted application operations.

The preparation module validates one versioned `SceneSpec` and resolves every factual reference before any resource acquisition or renderer allocation. Deterministic local templates produce that same schema. Its output has bounded geometry/layout descriptors, inert application-owned labels/legends, exact metric/evidence references, typed pick targets, safe reading descriptors, current resource requests and declared budgets. It contains no raw rows, complete source document, physical reader object or blob URL. Layout seed and template/calculation versions are explicit. The same valid input and seed produce the same positions/targets without changing a fact.

Reuse a small concrete Three.js controller through the existing component: mount a validated scene, update current input/selection, focus its exact target, pause/resume and dispose. Final lifecycle names may remain private to that renderer. Browser APIs, camera/orbit/pointer/resize operations and owned GPU/media handles live there or in the existing browser adapter. Do not add a rendering plugin registry, generic scene graph DSL, provider layer, shared mutable scene store or a second worker framework.

Picking returns a typed target from the prepared input. The application validates it against current authority before resolving an evidence or navigation action; the renderer never builds a route from a raw source path. HTML buttons use the identical target resolver. The existing MEDIA/COVERAGE interfaces perform exact reads with their own before/after authority checks.

Costly layout preparation uses the existing bounded job/worker adapter under assigned ownership. Layout transforms consume approved values, stable target IDs and fixed policy only. Counts, membership, occurrence grouping, calendar bins and language statistics stay in the accepted domain modules. A renderer may map a validated value onto height/area/color under a declared scale, but cannot calculate a new population, denominator or relationship.

## Validation, authority and failure

Freeze supported schema/template versions and the finite enum of room, metric, style, layout and camera intents. Reject unknown fields, unsupported types, invalid numeric bounds, duplicate/dangling targets, contradictory field proof, stale primary/metric authority and incomplete/oversized proposals. Use fixed safe errors. Treat supplied DTOs as inert data; validation must not execute accessors, user callbacks, arbitrary `toString` or coercion hooks. No arbitrary HTML, JavaScript, shader, import path, remote URL, factual numeric override or model-created record ID is allowed. A label resolves approved local text or a metric reference, not an unverified numeric claim.

The authority includes the exact session/fingerprint/normalization/occurrence/ordering/base-evidence and committed dataset/query/review/collection state, canonical query/timezone, template/layout version and the actual metric's parameters/calculation versions. Comparison revision/bounds/sub-scopes belong only to comparison-dependent inputs, jobs, selections and evidence cursors. A new or refused comparison cannot retag/recompute/invalidate the unchanged primary timeline, ranking, word page, coverage packet or valid base job. Old comparison inputs/cursors cease to be current. One immutable prepared input/set can retain unchanged primary descriptors; no second mutable store is necessary.

Equivalent shared-filter input or failed/canceled preparation preserves the coherent committed scene/evidence/leases. During a pending FILTER operation the old committed scope remains current; only actual commit invalidates relevant old work. After a real primary commit or archive replacement, obsolete scene inputs, labels, picking indexes, source actions and resources are withheld before the new authority is presented. Late workers, bitmap/texture callbacks, initializers and recovery attempts cannot attach old data. Input rejection cannot fabricate a zero measurement or make an old comparison current fallback.

Invalid/unsupported local or optional model proposals fall back to the deterministic template for the current valid packets. If those packets themselves are missing/stale/invalid, show the corresponding unavailable/reading state rather than a fabricated template. Optional runtime AI scene planning is not enabled by this task. It requires Phase 1's exact inspected packet, distinct transfer/provider consent, funded owner connection, cancellation/usage and truthful unavailable state. There is no shared paid fallback, native-mesh assumption, automatic retry or scripted result.

## Recorded-location limits

Coordinate parsing requires actual supported occurrence field proof. The narrow existing literal grammar may validate latitude/longitude within their finite bounds; out-of-range values are invalid, not clamped. A text place name, unsupported object shape, absent value and invalid pair are distinct limitation states. A physical-file location accumulated from another appearance supplies no location proof. No map, tile, terrain, geocoder, fonts or remote image endpoint receives location, viewport, media URL, archive ID or query. Base geometry is local and reproducible.

Recorded decimal places describe the literal representation, not GPS accuracy. Unknown datum/accuracy remains unknown. Draw a confidence area only if a supported export field actually records an accuracy quantity, unit and source-backed interpretation under a separately frozen rule. Without that evidence, use an explicit accuracy-unknown legend, never a guessed radius. A location grouping states that it combines equal recorded coordinates; it does not claim a shared venue or visit. Each contributing occurrence remains inspectable with its own time/source.

Unknown-time points are still recorded locations, with no invented timed route or calendar bin. Date-only preserves its original date and does not acquire midnight. Layout/culling may change presentation, never move a point to an inferred city. Filtered/denied occurrence locations and exclusive source proof disappear from both coordinate buffers and labels even when the original file remains permitted through another appearance.

## GPU lifecycle and capability

Prefer real WebGPU capability where available and verify actual WebGL 2 fallback. Report the renderer actually allocated and drawn, not merely the presence of `navigator.gpu` or a requested adapter. Initial allocation, async device/renderer initialization, resize, draw, error callbacks and device/context loss belong to a generation/authority owner. A late initializer after exit/replacement is disposed and cannot create a new context, canvas or data attachment.

Keep the existing bounded one-recovery policy: preserve committed collection, room, selected target, valid evidence page and motion state; release the failed backend; attempt a current-input rebuild on WebGL 2; then enter the complete reading journey if recovery fails. Device loss does not cancel valid archive/profile/export authority or silently change filters. No unbounded recreation loop or false backend label. Visible data after recovery must still pass current selection/resource authority, including a filter commit occurring during recovery.

Room exit, archive replacement, reset and renderer replacement release their owned geometry, materials, textures, render targets/buffers, controls, resize/visibility/pointer/loss listeners, animation frames, canvases and staged resources. MEDIA releases originating URLs/bitmaps/decoder leases. Cleanup is idempotent and continues after partial allocation/disposal failures. Duplicate/shared resources are released once when their last owned use ends. Failed decode/upload cannot leave a pick target for an unavailable/withheld thumbnail. Known metadata and source selection remain usable.

Observe owned resources and lifecycle peaks separately from measured browser/process memory. Garbage collection or driver release timing is not a deterministic zero-heap claim. Repeated room switches, recovery and reimports cannot grow owned contexts, listeners, active decodes, URLs or resident scene media without bound.

Before code, bind buffer peaks across current and staged uploads and the finite ownership of actual pending initialization/recovery. Native work that ignores cancellation remains charged until it settles; a stale generation cannot release its real lifetime slot early. Failed texture decoding withholds that texture while retaining a current permitted occurrence/metadata evidence action. These resource/target bindings are prerequisites, not measured guarantees from this design.
